Connect your phone

  1. Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as this PC — or turn on the phone's hotspot and connect the PC to it. No internet needed.
  2. Scan this QR code with the phone's camera.

Phone can't open the page?
  • Check both devices are on the same network.
  • Windows may block it: when Windows Firewall asks, allow Node.js on Private and Public networks, or run firewall.bat as administrator once.
  • If you see several addresses above, try another one.
  • Some routers have "AP/client isolation" on guest Wi-Fi — use the phone hotspot instead.
