Connect your phone

  1. 1Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as this PC — or turn on the phone's hotspot and connect the PC to it. No internet needed.
  2. 2Open the phone camera and point it at the QR code.

Phone can't open the page?
  • Check both devices are on the same network.
  • Windows may block it: when Windows Firewall asks, allow Node.js on Private and Public networks, or run firewall.bat as administrator once.
  • If you see several addresses above, tap another one.
  • Guest Wi-Fi often blocks devices from seeing each other — use a hotspot instead.
