PC ↔ phone.
No internet.

Throw files, photos, videos and links between your computer and your phone over Wi-Fi or a hotspot. Scan a QR code and you're in — nothing to install on the phone.

Download for PC One-line install

Fully offline

Works on any Wi-Fi router or phone hotspot. Mobile data can stay off.

Any size, full speed

Files stream straight to disk at your network's speed. 10 GB movie? Fine.

No phone app

The phone just uses its browser. Android or iPhone, it doesn't matter.

Private

Files never touch the internet, and an access code keeps neighbours out.

How it works

  1. Install it on your PC — download below, or use the one-line installer.
  2. Open ApOffSend from the desktop shortcut (or double-click start.bat). A page with a QR code opens.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone camera while both are on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
  4. Send away — drop files on either side and they appear on the other.
